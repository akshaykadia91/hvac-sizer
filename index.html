Pipe Sizer

  1. Open Design basis and pick chilled or condenser water, the pipe material (MS Class C, GI Class B / C or uPVC), supply and return water temperature.
  2. Set the max friction and max velocity, or tap a preset.
  3. Size a pipe: type the load in TR, kW or a flow. The smallest pipe that stays inside both limits is shown.
  4. Capacity table: the maximum TR every pipe size can carry.

Duct Sizer

  1. In Design basis choose the duct material, air temperature, site altitude and CFM per TR.
  2. Set the friction and velocity limits, the rectangular size step, max aspect ratio and, for tight ceilings, max duct height.
  3. Size: enter the airflow. You get the best rectangular size (least sheet metal within your rules) and the round size.
  4. Round and Chart list the max airflow of every size. Convert changes rectangular to round and back.

Reading the colours

Green: comfortably inside the limit.
Orange: above 85 % of the limit, little spare capacity.
Red: over the limit, the size is too small.

The SI / IP switch at the top changes the result units. Each input keeps its own unit and converts when you change it.

Typical design values

  • Chilled and condenser water, steel pipe: friction 1.5 to 4 ft wc/100 ft (150 to 400 Pa/m), velocity up to 2.4 to 3 m/s in large mains, 1.2 to 1.5 m/s near quiet rooms.
  • uPVC pipe: keep velocity near 1.5 m/s and check the pressure and temperature rating.
  • Low-pressure supply duct: 0.06 to 0.1 in wc/100 ft. Mains 1200 to 1800 fpm, branches 600 to 1000 fpm.
  • Comfort air conditioning: 350 to 450 CFM per TR. Aspect ratio up to 4 : 1.
  • GI sheet gauge by larger side (IS 655 / CPWD): up to 750 mm 24 G, to 1500 mm 22 G, to 2250 mm 20 G, above that 18 G.

These are common practice. Follow your project specification.

How it is calculated

  • Friction: Darcy-Weisbach with the Colebrook friction factor and the pipe or duct roughness.
  • Water density, viscosity and specific heat at the mean of supply and return temperature. Air density from temperature and altitude.
  • Chilled water flow per TR = 3.517 kW ÷ (density × specific heat × ΔT). Condenser water is multiplied by the heat rejection factor.
  • Pipe bores: MS / GI Class C = IS 1239 Heavy, GI Class B = IS 1239 Medium, MS DN 200 and above = IS 3589, uPVC = Schedule 40.
  • Rectangular ducts use the Huebscher equivalent diameter De = 1.30 (a·b)0.625 ÷ (a+b)0.25.

1 TR = 3.517 kW · 1 L/s = 15.85 USgpm · 1 CFM = 0.4719 L/s · 1 ft wc/100 ft = 98.07 Pa/m · 1 in wc/100 ft = 8.17 Pa/m
